1. HOW TO INSTALL & RUN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[WINDOWS PC USERS]
Option A - Standard Installer:
  1. Download 'A2Z-Downloader-Setup.exe'.
  2. Double-click the file to install.
  3. Once installed, an 'A2Z Downloader' icon will appear on your Desktop.
  4. Launch the app and start downloading or editing!

Option B - Direct Run (Portable):
  If using the direct unpacked version from GitHub, run 'Run-A2Z.bat' or 
  'A2Z Downloader.exe' inside the folder without installation.

[ANDROID PHONE USERS]
  1. Download 'A2Z-Downloader.apk'.
  2. Tap the downloaded .apk file to install on your Android device.
  3. Allow 'Install from Unknown Sources' if prompted by Android security.
  4. Open the app to download videos directly into your phone's Gallery.

[WEB USERS]
  Visit: https://a2zdownloader.vercel.app (Accessible from Chrome, Brave, Safari, Edge)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. HOW TO DOWNLOAD VIDEOS & EXTRACT MP3
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Step 1: Copy any video link from YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, Instagram, Twitter/X,
          or direct web hosts.
  Step 2: Paste the link into the search bar in A2Z Downloader.
  Step 3: Choose your preferred format:
          - 1080p Full HD Video
          - 720p HD Video
          - Audio Only (320kbps MP3)
  Step 4: Click 'Download' — the file saves directly to your device's Downloads folder.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. HOW TO MAKE SHORT-FORM CLIPS IN STUDIO MODE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Step 1: On any analyzed video, click '✂️ Render Clip & Edit' (or go to Studio tab).
  Step 2: Choose your aspect ratio:
          - 9:16 (for TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels)
          - 16:9 (for YouTube and widescreen screens)
          - 1:1 (for square posts)
  Step 3: Set your start time and duration (e.g. 30s or 60s).
  Step 4: Turn on Kinetic Subtitles and pick a style (Hormozi yellow, TikTok white, Neon cyan).
  Step 5: Add a headline banner title at the top if desired.
  Step 6: Click 'Render & Export Video (MP4)'.

5. CONS & CURRENT LIMITATIONS (PLEASE READ BEFORE DOWNLOADING)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  ⚠ 4K Ultra HD on very long videos:
    Downloading 4K on long videos (15 to 30+ minutes) can be heavy or may fail
    depending on YouTube's adaptive DASH rate limits. For best stability, 
    the app downloads mostly in 1080p Full HD and 720p HD.
  
  ⚠ Auto-Captions:
    Subtitles and captions are generated from available speech/subtitle tracks. 
    Some videos without clear speech, music-only tracks, or unsupported languages 
    may not generate auto-captions automatically.
  
  ⚠ Studio Manual Zoom:
    Custom manual zoom scaling inside 9:16 vertical mode is currently being fine-tuned
    in upcoming patch updates. The standard 9:16 center-cut and blurred padding 
    work normally.
